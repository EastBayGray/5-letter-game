Alphabet

Tap a letter once to cross it off, twice to put it in the circle, and a third time to clear it.

You can also drag a letter straight onto the circle. Tap a letter in the circle to take it out.

Hide takes the crossed-off and circled letters out of the alphabet, leaving only the ones you haven't worked out yet. Show all brings them back.

Scramble shuffles the letters in the circle so you can spot the word.

Each word length keeps its own game, so switching length won't lose your page.

My guesses

Type each word you ask them, then the number they tell you.

Tap a letter in a guess to cross it off. Tap again to circle it, and again to clear it. The alphabet updates too, and the mark shows in every guess with that letter.

Typo? Backspace while typing, or tap the line number to clear that line. Tap the little bin at the end of a line to delete it. A new line appears by itself when you start the last one.
