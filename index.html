Alphabet

Tap a letter once to cross it off, twice to put it in the circle, and a third time to clear it.

You can also drag a letter straight onto the circle. Tap a letter in the circle to take it out.

Hide takes the crossed-off and circled letters out of the alphabet, leaving only the ones you haven't worked out yet. Show all brings them back.

Scramble shuffles the letters in the circle so you can spot the word.

Each word length keeps its own game, so switching length won't lose your page.

Play online: pick a length, tap Play online, and share the code. The other person taps Join a game and types it in. Whoever picks their secret word first goes first. Your score against each person is kept on this phone. Your guesses send themselves once they're a real word, the numbers fill in on their own, and you can change your secret word until the first guess.

Tap a letter in a guess to cross it off. Tap again to circle it, and again to clear it. The alphabet updates too, and the mark shows in every guess with that letter.

Typo? Backspace while typing, or tap the line number to clear that line. If it isn't a real word, the letters go red with a dashed line and you can't give it a number. Tap the little bin at the end of a line to delete it. A new line appears by itself when you start the last one.
